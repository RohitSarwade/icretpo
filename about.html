About Our Campus Placement Portal

Know more about our college, placement cell and this website.

About Our Institute

Our college is more than 30 years old and provides engineering and management courses. We have good labs, experienced teachers and good campus facilities. Many of our students get placed in top companies every year. We are proud of our alumni who are working all over India and abroad.

About Placement Cell (TPO)

The Training and Placement Office helps students get jobs. They call companies to our campus and organize placement drives. They also conduct mock interviews and aptitude test practice for students. The TPO team works hard throughout the year to get good companies for us.

About This Portal

This portal is made so that students can easily see which companies are coming for placement. You can check the package, role, CGPA requirement and drive date on the companies page. You can also register yourself and login to apply for companies. The results will also be shown here after the drive.


Features of this portal:

- Company listings with open/closed status

- Student registration and login

- Placement results page

- Notify Me option for upcoming drives
